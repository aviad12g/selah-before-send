Mara writes, “I’m tired of you pretending this isn’t a choice. Stop calling it complicated.”
You fire back, “You have no idea what I was carrying.” Your cursor reaches Send.

Tap Pause.

This is Selah Before Send—a private pause before that reply leaves your hands.
First, Gloo reads the heat—not who is right. Here it sees defensiveness, and a need to be understood. That selects a pre-approved theme.
YouVersion returns the exact passage and its wider context. The model never chooses or invents Scripture.

Then Selah asks one private question. It never writes the reply, and it never posts for you.
Edit in your own words. Wait ten minutes. Or send anyway. Your voice—and your choice—stay yours.

And if the message is more than angry, Selah does not answer a crisis with a verse.

It stops.

No Scripture retrieval. No reflection. It points toward immediate human help.

Every step has a fixed structure, and if anything fails, the system stops safely.
Gloo classifies, YouVersion retrieves, and Gloo reflects using only the returned text.

Selah doesn’t ask technology to speak for you.
It gives you one quiet moment to decide who you want to be—before your words leave your hands.
