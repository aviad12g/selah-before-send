# Selah Before Send — video script and shot list

Target runtime: 2:40–2:55. Keep the product screen visible for most of the film; this is a demonstration, not a concept montage.

## 0:00–0:15 — The one-second problem

**Picture:** Black screen. A reply cursor blinks. The sample reply appears word by word. The pointer hovers over Send.

**Narration:**
“Some of the most consequential moments online last one second: the distance between writing something angry and pressing send. What if Scripture could meet us inside that second?”

## 0:15–0:38 — The premise

**Picture:** Pull back to reveal the Selah composer and the line “private-facing · never auto-posts.” Slowly frame the two send choices and the visible Gloo-processing disclosure.

**Narration:**
“Selah Before Send is an opt-in pause inside a familiar social reply. It is not another Bible destination, and it does not broadcast a verse at someone else. It creates one private-facing beat to listen, reflect, and choose again.”

## 0:38–1:18 — Open the pause

**Picture:** Click **Pause before sending**. Show the result panel, conversation temperature, underlying need, passage, reference, and version. If the deployment lacks credentials, hold for one second on the visible **CURATED OFFLINE PREVIEW** label.

**Narration:**
“Here, a tense post has triggered a defensive draft. Selah first reads the temperature and describes the need beneath the words charitably: to be understood before being judged. It then surfaces James 1—not text generated by a model, but an identified passage with its version and attribution.”

**Recording truth gate:** If live credentials have been configured and the path has passed an end-to-end test, say “This result is running through the live API path.” Otherwise say: “This review build uses a clearly labeled curated fixture; the live integration is implemented but not yet credential-validated.”

## 1:18–1:42 — Context, not proof-texting

**Picture:** Click **Read passage context**. Scroll just enough to show the surrounding verses and attribution.

**Narration:**
“A single line can be misused when it is detached from its setting. Selah makes the wider passage one tap away, so reflection starts with context rather than a floating quotation.”

## 1:42–2:12 — Agency remains with the user

**Picture:** Reveal the private question. Click **Edit in my own words** and show the three editing moves. Then reset, click **Pause 10 minutes**, show the countdown ticking, and briefly show **Send anyway**.

**Narration:**
“The question never enters the public conversation. No model text is inserted into the reply. Selah offers three choices: edit in my own words, start a ten-minute countdown, or send anyway. The final language and decision remain with the person who typed them. This prototype is not connected to a social account, so nothing is posted or persisted by the app.”

## 2:12–2:40 — Show the provenance chain

**Picture:** Pan across the on-page three-step architecture strip. Add simple labels over the existing cards: Gloo → YouVersion → Gloo.

**Narration:**
“Underneath is a constrained, two-stage AI pipeline. Gloo classifies a possible tension into a fixed schema. YouVersion retrieves an exact passage, context, and attribution from a pinned Bible. A second Gloo request is instructed to use that supplied text as its only Biblical grounding. Strict output contracts, a pre-AI safety stop, and fail-closed errors keep provenance visible.”

## 2:40–2:55 — The frontier

**Picture:** Return to the blinking cursor, now beside **Pause before sending**. End on the wordmark.

**Narration:**
“The frontier is not always a new screen. Sometimes it is a better choice inside the screen we already use. Selah Before Send: Scripture present before our words leave our hands.”

## Recording checklist

- Replace the truth-gate line only after confirming which mode the deployed demo actually reports.
- Do not claim behavior change, conflict reduction, or user adoption; no study has been run.
- If live mode is shown, disclose that Gloo processes the post and draft server-side.
- Show the product working in one uninterrupted sequence.
- Keep the final upload public, no-login, and under three minutes.
- Add captions and ensure API/version attribution remains legible at 1080p.
